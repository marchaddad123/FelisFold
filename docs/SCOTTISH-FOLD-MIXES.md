# Scottish Fold mixes: evidence and publication decisions

Research review: 1 October 2026. Names describe reported ancestry, not newly established breeds. Crossbreeding does not guarantee removal of TRPV4. Genotype and clinical severity are separate questions. The library supports existing owners, not a breeding programme.

General context: [UC Davis TRPV4 test](https://vgl.ucdavis.edu/test/scottish-fold), [2021 radiographic study](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/34406467/), [2023 evaluation](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10811760/). Case reports are evidence that an outcome exists, not that every cat has that outcome.

No additional designer names, assumed breed photographs or fabricated cat stories were added. Future profiles require a defensible source or a documented owner case, with the same explicit uncertainty.

The [2026 Japanese survey](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC13158167/) adds variant detection in reported Norwegian Forest and Minuet cats. It suggests admixture but does not confirm parents or clinical outcomes. These are research leads, not additional confirmed-cross profiles.
