## Editorial safeguards

- Each general medical article names its sources and editorial review date. Owner panels are explicitly labelled and have no citations implying research validates Lotus's own history.
- Mobility and vomiting use the same sequence: explanation, signs, mimics, safe home actions, avoid, vet contact, investigation, management.
- Emergency guidance names its source and links the reader to the list. No reassuring timer or promise that a vet is unnecessary.
- Domperidone/Motilium is owner history only. No tablet fraction or dosing recommendation is published.
- Pedigree and mix labels do not verify genotype. The two published mixed cases were homozygous, not representative prevalence samples.
- Homemade entries remain empty until measured owner records arrive. Complete-diet claims require professional formulation; no nutrient arithmetic or chicken-only recipe is offered.
- No new external images were downloaded. Existing owner-supplied photography is reused. No random cat photograph is labelled as a documented cross.

## Records to request from Mark when available

Imaging report/type and dates; veterinary assessment notes; pain medicine name and prescription; Motilium strength/prescription history; exact Eminent product label; neutering and Kinder dates; older weights; meal dates, preparation, ingredient/portion/eaten grams and follow-up observations. These gaps do not prevent publishing honest owner history.

Additional research: [Ukawa et al., Animal Genetics, May 2026](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC13158167/): genetic survey; supports variant distribution beyond recorded Folds. Japanese sampling, reported breed labels and no clinical follow-up limit interpretation. Used in the mixes overview, with no universal risk estimate.

[Anderson et al., PLOS Genetics, June 2022](https://journals.plos.org/plosgenetics/article?id=10.1371/journal.pgen.1009804): primary genetic study documenting a Fold × Highlander kitten; used in that profile. Single-case outcome limits apply. [CFA breed history](https://cfa.org/breed/scottish-fold/): registry history reference for Susie/1961, not medical guidance; undated online page accessed October 2026, used only for breed origin.
